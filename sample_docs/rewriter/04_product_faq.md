## Product: Celadon Jade Rice Bowl — 翡翠茶碗

---

### Q1: Is this bowl dishwasher safe?

Yes. We design every piece for daily use — that includes the dishwasher. Our celadon glaze is fired at 1300°C, making it highly durable. We do recommend avoiding abrasive detergents to preserve the jade whisper finish over time.

### Q2: Why does each bowl look slightly different?

Because each bowl is hand-thrown on the wheel by a single artisan — we never use molds. Subtle variations in form, brushstroke, and glaze depth are a hallmark of authentic handcraft, not imperfections.

### Q3: What is the celadon 'jade whisper' finish?

It's our proprietary celadon glaze, formulated by our founder in 1923 and unchanged since. When fired at 1300°C for 36 hours, it develops a soft, translucent jade-green surface that deepens subtly with use.

### Q4: Where is the clay sourced?

We use Amakusa clay from Kumamoto Prefecture — one of Japan's finest porcelain clays. It's hand-wedged for 30 minutes before throwing to ensure a silken, air-free body.

### Q5: How long does it take to make one bowl?

Each piece goes through 23 individual steps over approximately 6 weeks, from clay wedging to final kiln firing. We don't rush craft.

### Q6: Can I use this for microwave heating?

Yes, our porcelain is microwave safe. The high-fire celadon glaze contains no metallic compounds. However, like all ceramics, avoid sudden temperature changes (e.g., freezer to microwave).
